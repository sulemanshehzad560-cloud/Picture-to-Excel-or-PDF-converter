# OmniScan privacy policy

_Last updated: 30 September 2026_

OmniScan turns photos and scans of documents into Excel, Word, PDF and other files.

**Everything happens on your device.** The Android app has no internet permission, so it
cannot send your images, text or files anywhere.

- **Images you scan** are processed in memory on your phone and are not uploaded.
- **Recent scans** (the recognised text and a small preview) are stored only on your device,
  inside the app, so you can reopen them. Delete them from the Recent scans screen, or by
  clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.
- **Exported files** are saved where you choose (Documents/OmniScan, or any app you pick from
  the share sheet). OmniScan does not read them afterwards.
- **Camera**: used only when you tap "Take photo", through your phone's camera app.
- **No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no third-party SDKs that collect data.**

The optional self-hosted web server (for developers) can use an AI service only if its operator
configures an API key. The Android app never does.
