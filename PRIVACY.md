# OmniScan privacy policy

_Last updated: 1 October 2026_

OmniScan turns photos and scans of documents into Excel, Word, PDF and other files.

**Your documents never leave your device.** Scanning, reading and exporting all happen on your
phone. OmniScan does not upload your images, the recognised text or your exported files.

- **Images you scan** are processed in memory on your phone and are not uploaded.
- **Recent scans** (the recognised text and a small preview) are stored only on your device,
  inside the app, so you can reopen them. Delete them from the Recent scans screen, or by
  clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.
- **Exported files** are saved where you choose (Documents/OmniScan, or any app you pick from
  the share sheet). OmniScan does not read them afterwards.
- **Camera**: used only when you tap "Take photo", through your phone's camera app.
- **No accounts and no analytics.**

## Advertising
OmniScan is free and shows one small banner ad at the bottom of the screen, served by
**Google AdMob**. The internet permission is used only for these ads and Google's consent form.
- To show and measure ads, Google's ads SDK may collect your device's **advertising ID**, your
  approximate location (from your IP address), app interaction and diagnostic data. How Google
  uses this data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
- In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland (and where else the law requires it) we ask for your
  consent first with Google's consent form. You can change your choice any time with
  **Settings → Ad privacy choices**.
- You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's
  **Settings → Google → Ads** (or **Settings → Privacy → Ads**).
- Ads never see your scans, the recognised text or your files.

We do not sell your data. Your documents are never shared with advertisers or anyone else.

The optional self-hosted web server (for developers) can use an AI service only if its operator
configures an API key. The Android app never does.
